How to make kefir

Four steps to your first glass.

No machines and no powders. Just milk, a jar and your kefir seeds.

Add

Put the kefir seeds in a clean glass jar.

Pour

Cover them with fresh milk.

Wait

Cover loosely and leave at room temperature for about a day.

Strain

Strain, enjoy your kefir, and start again with the same seeds.
